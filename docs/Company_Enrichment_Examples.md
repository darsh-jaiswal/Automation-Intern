Funding and headcount figures are historical statements as of their publication dates. They are not represented as the latest round, current valuation, current headcount, available budget or willingness to buy Myntmore's services.

`company_enrichment.json` shows a reusable record format: company domain, dated fact, source URL and retrieval date. In a production extension, match records by verified company domain, preserve the source and fact date, and show unknown values as unknown. The assignment's `.example` domains cannot be enriched as real companies, so these examples are deliberately separate. Automatic company matching and scheduled refreshes are not implemented.
